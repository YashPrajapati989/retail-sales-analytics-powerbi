# Retail Sales Analytics | Power BI

## 📊 Project Overview

An end-to-end retail sales analytics project designed to transform
transactional sales data into actionable business insights.

The project analyses sales performance, customer behaviour,
product performance and operational activity using SQL, Python
and Power BI.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The objective is to help management understand:

- Overall sales performance
- Customer value and retention opportunities
- Product revenue contribution
- Geographic sales concentration
- Operational demand
- Areas requiring business intervention

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Champions generate approximately **72.1% of total revenue**
- **1,912 customers** are classified as At Risk or Need Attention
- **960 Potential Loyalists** represent a significant growth opportunity
- Revenue is highly concentrated in the UK market
- A relatively small number of products contribute a substantial share of revenue

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

### Protect Champions
Use VIP rewards and personalised offers to retain high-value customers.

### Convert Potential Loyalists
Use cross-selling and loyalty incentives to increase repeat purchases.

### Re-engage At Risk Customers
Launch personalised reactivation campaigns.

### Optimise Inventory
Prioritise stock availability for high-revenue products.

### Expand Geographic Reach
Explore opportunities beyond the highly concentrated UK market.
